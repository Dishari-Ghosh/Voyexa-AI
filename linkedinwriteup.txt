✈️ **I didn’t want to build just another travel website.**

I wanted to build something that could answer a slightly different question:

**“Is this destination actually suitable for me?”**

And that idea became **Voyexa AI** — my AI-powered travel recommendation and planning platform focused on exploring India. 🇮🇳

While building it, I realized that travel planning isn't simply about finding a beautiful place.

A destination that is perfect for one traveller may not necessarily be the right choice for another.

So I built Voyexa AI around the idea of **context-aware travel recommendations**.

🌍 **What can you do with Voyexa AI?**

→ Explore **1,163 destinations across India**

→ Search and filter destinations by state, zone and interests

→ Discover lesser-known **hidden gems**

→ Consider traveller preferences and budget

→ Take **traveller age** into account when evaluating destination suitability

→ Get personalized destination recommendations

→ Explore destinations using interactive maps

→ Interact with an integrated **Gemini AI travel assistant**

→ Build a personalized trip

→ Generate a structured itinerary

→ Export the itinerary as a **PDF**

But one of the things I'm most proud of isn't actually a particular feature.

It is the **dataset behind the project.**

The dataset of 1,163 destinations was **created and structured by me specifically for this project.**

That meant working on destination information, categorization, attributes, interests, zones, hidden-gem identification and the data required to make the recommendation system meaningful.

And the project is **still evolving.**

🖼️ I'm continuing to add and organize destination images across the website, and more place-specific visuals will be added step by step as I keep developing the platform.

So what you're seeing today isn't the final version.

It's a product that is still being built, refined and improved.

💡 **The idea behind Voyexa AI is simple:**

Instead of only asking:

**“Where can I travel?”**

I wanted the system to move toward:

**“Where makes sense for *me* to travel?”**

For example, a destination involving strenuous terrain may be a great recommendation for one traveller but may need to be treated differently for an older traveller.

That small observation influenced how I designed the recommendation experience.
